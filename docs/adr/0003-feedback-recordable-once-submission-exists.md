# Feedback is recordable once a Submission exists, not only after the deadline

A Supervisor may record Feedback for a Milestone as soon as the owning Team has a Submission, whether or not the deadline has passed. We chose this over restricting Feedback to after the Submission cutoff because supervision feedback is often most useful before the deadline, and because it lets the full domain journey (create milestone, submit, give feedback, reach Completed) be demonstrated and tested without waiting for a deadline to elapse. Feedback remains a single immutable entry without a score, associated with the current Submission at the time it is recorded. Milestone status becomes Completed once Feedback exists regardless of the deadline; a Missed Milestone still cannot receive Feedback because it has no Submission. A past-deadline seeded Milestone (and an injectable clock in tests) is still required to exercise the Submission cutoff and the Missed status.
